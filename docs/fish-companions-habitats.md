The table expands the shipped home tags: `is_ocean` includes frozen oceans and therefore makes a species cold hardy under the current any-cold-home rule. This is an explicit review point for species with broad ocean biome weights. Cherry grove and mangrove swamp are temperate. Data packs can change runtime holder membership; the loaded profile registry remains authoritative. A missing time multiplier is neutral. Water, light and music remain available to every species.

Owner review remains an explicit release gate.
