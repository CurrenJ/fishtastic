# Product direction and backlog

Fishing is the experience: quests encourage deliberate catches, rewards change the
minigame's feel or improve the display, and prestige cosmetics never gate basic play.
Avoid mandatory crafting chains that consume the player's fish. These are ideas,
not scheduled work or implementation contracts. Recheck current content before
starting one; code and the system references describe what already ships.

## Fishing and progression ideas

- Permanent quest-reward rod upgrades: temperament phase insight, faster impulses
  with a drain tradeoff, or temporary selective target assistance. One rod with
  accumulated upgrades was the proposed direction; storage and packet designs
  should be chosen when implementation is authorized.
- Special consumable bait: relaxed/no-drain sessions, a guaranteed special reward,
  or two bobbers controlled by one impulse. Preserve meaningful tradeoffs rather
  than only increasing numeric power.
- Void/lava rods; a two-part oarfish encounter; moray eel; unusually large catches;
  rare fish evading the visible bar and returning elsewhere; a species-changing
  fish moon; richer treasure; rotating lucky days; a decaying banana charm.
- More daily variety, competitive daily/weekly leaderboards, an auto-reeler with
  an explicit hook tradeoff, a Calm Bait "Zen Garden" quest, and progression tied
  to other world achievements if it still reinforces fishing.
- Posed-player leaderboard displays, a free daily shop reroll, permanently unlocked
  box-of-fish recipes, and banner notifications that respect vanilla toast queues.
  Treat these as proposals until their current implementation has been checked.

## Tank ideas

- Wall-mounted barnacles, sponges, coral, lichen and portholes; upside-down coral
  needs a clear placement distinction from floor coral.
- Shape-aware cosmetic floor bounds for tapered tanks; review existing collision
  and occupied-cell rules before introducing another footprint model.
- Actual scene lighting, god rays, bubble stones/curtains, black smokers, anemones,
  floating plants, varied grass/kelp, modular ruins, helmets/anchors, fossils,
  terraces, shell scatters, coral kits, sea-glass floors and a tire swing.
- Benthic dens, perch/graze, cleaning stations and feeding rings, without turning
  cosmetic behavior into hunger or maintenance requirements.
- Approved brainstorm directions needing new reaction primitives: an armillary
  sphere needs accumulated rotation/open-gate pass events; a black smoker needs a
  clock without a nearby fish; a diver's helmet valve needs an on-exit trigger;
  a cleaning station needs fish perching.

Tall kelp, ceiling cosmetics, spans, hiding shelters and reactive clam/chest/shrine
structures already ship. Species lore is populated. The distinct-bait collector
quest and bait-buddy reward exist. Do not turn those old checklist entries into
new implementation tasks. Pending owner reviews stay beside their system's
contract, not in this speculative backlog.
